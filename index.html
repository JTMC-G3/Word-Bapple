How to play
  1. Everyone takes turns naming a word that starts with the letter in the bubble. Starting with A.
  2. No repeats. If a word was already said this letter, it doesn't count.
  3. Run out of time, or press I'm stuck, and you're out.
  4. As soon as one person is out, the letter moves on: A, then B, then C...
  5. Think a word is fake? Hit Challenge. If the group agrees, whoever said it is out.
  6. Last player standing wins!
