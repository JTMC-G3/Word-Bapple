How to play
  1. Take turns naming a word that starts with the letter in the bubble, starting with A.
  2. It has to be a real English word, and no repeats on the same letter.
  3. Run out of time, or press I'm stuck, and you're out.
  4. As soon as one person is out, the letter moves on: A, then B, then C…
  5. Last player standing wins!
